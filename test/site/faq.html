Frequently asked questions about Example Analytics

These answers cover pricing, hosting and data ownership for Example Analytics, the self-hosted product analytics tool from Example Ltd.

FAQ

Can I self-host Example Analytics?

Yes. Example Analytics runs on Docker or Kubernetes on your own servers.

Where is my data stored?

Your data stays on the servers you run it on.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $49 per month.
